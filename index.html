How to read these numbers

Combat weight drives how much damage you take from a hit. Heavier dinosaurs shrug off attacks from lighter ones, so it matters as much as health.

Colors: carnivores are tinted red and herbivores green, and each square shows the dinosaur's in-game character icon.

Speeds show in Game units (cm/s) by default, the number players usually quote, such as an 800 sprint for Tyrannosaurus. Switch to km/h or mph any time.

Rank on a dinosaur page compares it with every dinosaur in the current list. A small "lower is better" note marks stats such as stamina costs and turn radius.

Live hotfixes: Alderon sometimes changes values on official servers without a full patch. When that has happened for a dinosaur, its page lists the affected values.

Community servers can override any stat in their server settings, and modded dinosaurs change whenever their mod updates. Treat numbers on modded or custom servers as a starting point.

Sharing: every dinosaur has its own page address (for example potdinos.com/dinosaurs/tyrannosaurus) that opens straight to it.

Fight matchup: tap Fight matchup in the filter bar, pick 2 to 4 dinosaurs (the one you play first), then tap Compare. You get every stat next to theirs, how many hits each needs to kill the others and who wins a straight trade of attacks.

Want the bigger picture? The Guides explain growth, combat, status effects, nesting and more, and the stats guide has a damage calculator.

How to use the map

  • Move: drag, or use the arrow keys.
  • Zoom: scroll, pinch or double-click, or press + and −. Press 0 to see the whole map.
  • Show more: a new visit shows only the region names. Switch on Landmarks, Flights or any item types in the Items tab; your choices are saved on this device for each map until you switch them off.
  • Details: click or tap any icon, landmark or region name.
  • Progress: “Mark as found” saves on this device, and “Hide found” clears those spots from view.
  • Share: “Copy link” opens the map at that exact spot; “Copy coords” gives the in-game position for admin teleports.

Map images, markers and quests come from Potservers and are based on Path of Titans game files (© Alderon Games). This is an unofficial map; spots can move with game updates.
