How to read these numbers

Combat weight drives how much damage you take from a hit. Heavier creatures shrug off attacks from lighter ones, so it matters as much as health.

Speeds show in km/h by default. Switch to mph, or to Game units (cm/s), which is the number players usually quote, such as an 800 sprint for Tyrannosaurus.

Rank on a creature page compares it with every creature in the current list. A small "lower is better" note marks stats such as stamina costs and turn radius.

Live hotfixes: Alderon sometimes changes values on official servers without a full patch. When that has happened for a creature, its page lists the affected values.

Community servers can override any stat in their server settings, and modded creatures change whenever their mod updates. Treat numbers on modded or custom servers as a starting point.

Sharing: every creature has its own link (for example #tyrannosaurus) that opens straight to its page.

Stats and abilities come from the Potservers public API, which reads them from the game files and tracks live official-server hotfixes. This site re-checks it twice a day. Ability descriptions use in-game text; every number shown is a game value.
